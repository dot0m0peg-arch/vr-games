From link to headset

  1. Open this site in a WebXR-compatible browser on your VR headset, such as Meta Quest Browser.
  2. Choose a game from the collection. All are early access, and none has been tested on a headset yet.
  3. Use the game's Enter VR button and approve the browser's VR permission request. Each game will explain its own controls.

Check this browser

This checks for immersive VR support. It does not start a VR session or request headset permissions.
