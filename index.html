Training Room

A giant grassy field where you say what you need and it phases in: villas, axes, card tables, training dummies, whiteboards. Draw with the magic marker and the AI guesses what it is, then brings it to life.
Plays in any desktop or phone browser with typed commands; Enter VR appears on a WebXR headset (headset pass not yet verified). The local AI model, Whisper voice and laptop/phone sync need the free laptop bridge.
