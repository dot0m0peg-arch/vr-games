Controls and the first five minutes

Move with WASD. Mouse wheel changes camera distance; Q / R rotate the view. E opens a nearby container, speaks to a neighbor or opens a door. F examines a nearby body, including your own when no one is nearby. In care, select a tool and hold on the body; drag to cut or pull. Injuries bleed unless wrapped. Supplies belong to people. Witnesses remember taking them.

Hold B at an entry to fit a physical board, using wood. G takes a loose plank or grasps a seriously injured or dead body; walk to pull it, press G to let go. V enters/exits the car. WASD drives; the engine draws attention. N shows residents and their own memories. TAB opens your supplies. Rest, eat and drink through supplies. Autosaves retain bodies, resources and memories. Manual checkpoints survive later autosaves. Time pauses when this window loses focus.
